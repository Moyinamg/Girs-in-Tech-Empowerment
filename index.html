About This Hub

Tech can feel intimidating, especially when you're the only girl in the room. This hub gives you a space to feel seen, supported, and hyped up.

🌟

Why This Space?

Representation matters. Girls and non-binary students deserve to feel welcome in coding, robotics, cybersecurity, and every tech field.

🚀

Learn at Your Pace

No pressure, no judgment. Take your time with HTML, CSS, Python, robotics, or whatever you're curious about.
